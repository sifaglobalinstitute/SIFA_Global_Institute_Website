SIFA Application

Apply to learn, practice, and grow with SIFA.

A structured application process designed to understand your background, learning goals, preferred program, and any financial assistance you may wish to request.

Start your SIFA application

Complete the application step by step. SIFA determines program availability, cohort dates, and final timetables. You can review and edit your information before submission.

* Required field

Before you begin

Have your basic identification and education information available. Supporting documents can be added where applicable.

Important: Submitting an application does not guarantee admission, enrollment, a scholarship, or a fee waiver.

Personal Information

Education & Background

Help us understand your current level and relevant experience.

Program & Cohort

Select a program that is currently open for application and tell us your preferred learning arrangement.

Program availability is controlled by SIFA.

Only programs currently open for application can be selected. Other courses may be introduced in future cohorts. The selected program and final placement remain subject to SIFA review and availability.

Next Available Cohort

Applicants do not choose an exact start date. SIFA will confirm the next available cohort and official start date for the selected program.

Schedule Preference

Choose the type of schedule that suits you best. This is a preference only; SIFA will confirm the final timetable according to the selected program and cohort.

4

Scholarship & Financial Assistance

Tell us whether you want to be considered for financial assistance.

Important: SIFA may provide scholarships, discounts, or fee waivers when opportunities, funding, and eligibility conditions allow. A request is reviewed separately and does not guarantee an award.
5

Documents

Provide the core documents needed for application review and add supporting documents where useful.

Document policy: SIFA will request only documents relevant to the selected program or support request. The requirements below are designed for this application and may be adjusted when SIFA publishes program-specific admission requirements.

Core application documents Required

These documents support identity and education/qualification review. If you select the guidance option above, SIFA can advise you about an unavailable document.

Identification document / passportPassport or another accepted identification document.
Highest education or professional qualificationCertificate, diploma, degree, professional qualification, or equivalent record.

Optional / Supporting Documents Optional

These documents may strengthen your application or help SIFA understand your background. They are not required unless SIFA specifically requests them.

Academic transcript / recordUseful where academic background is relevant.
Passport-size photographRecent professional or identification-style photo.
CV / RésuméUseful for applicants with professional, volunteer, or business experience.
Professional or training certificatesRelevant accounting, finance, software, or technology certificates.
Work experience documentsRelevant employment or experience evidence, if available.
Other supporting documentsAny other relevant document you want SIFA to consider.

Accepted formats: PDF, JPG, JPEG, and PNG for most documents; PDF, DOC, or DOCX for CVs. Final file-size limits and secure storage rules will be applied when the online submission service is connected.

6

Review & Submit

Review the information you entered before the final submission stage.

Declaration & Consent

The application interface is ready, but the secure online submission and document-upload service has not yet been connected. We will connect the submission system before this page is used for real applications.
Application process: Complete the application → SIFA reviews the information → SIFA may contact you for clarification or supporting documents → enrollment arrangements are confirmed where applicable.

Before applying, you can also review Scholarships & Fee Waivers, Courses, or Contact SIFA.
