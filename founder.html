Leadership Behind SIFA Global Institute

Meet the leadership behind SIFA's vision of practical, technology-driven education in accounting, finance, and digital professional skills.

FOUNDER & CEO

Jean Bosco NKURIKIYUMUKIZA

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Jean Bosco NKURIKIYUMUKIZA is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of SIFA Global Institute, a technology-focused learning institute committed to practical education in accounting software, financial systems, and digital professional tools.

He has practical experience using accounting software and digital productivity tools, developed through training, professional practice, and continued learning.

He founded SIFA with the aim of helping learners connect accounting and financial knowledge with the technology used in modern professional environments.
