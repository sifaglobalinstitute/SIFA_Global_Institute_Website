Why SIFA

Accounting education should connect knowledge with the technology used in real work.

SIFA is designed around practical learning: understanding the accounting idea, practicing it with technology, and applying it through realistic tasks.

01

Understand

Connect accounting concepts to transactions, systems, reports, and business processes.

02

Practice

Work through realistic exercises using accounting software, spreadsheets, and digital tools.

03

Apply

Demonstrate what you have learned through tasks, projects, assessments, and practical applications.

The SIFA Learning Model

Learn the concept. Practice the process. Apply the skill.

SIFA focuses on practical competence rather than software demonstrations alone.

01

Concept

Understand the accounting or financial principle behind the task.

02

Software

Learn how the principle is handled inside a relevant digital system.

03

Practice

Work with realistic transactions, business situations, and exercises.

04

Demonstrate

Show practical ability through assignments, projects, or assessments.
