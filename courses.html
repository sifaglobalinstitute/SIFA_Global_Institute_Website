The SIFA Learning Model

SIFA is designed around practical application. Learners should understand the accounting or business concept, practice the workflow using appropriate technology, and then apply the skill in realistic professional situations.

01 — Learn Understand the concept, terminology, workflow, and purpose behind the task.
02 — Practice Work through guided exercises using realistic accounting and business scenarios.
03 — Apply Perform the workflow independently and interpret the resulting financial information.
04 — Develop Continue building professional technology skills through advanced tools and systems.
Program availability: SIFA Global Institute is developing its learning portfolio in stages. Programs marked as Current Focus represent the present training direction, while Coming Soon and Future Program areas represent planned curriculum expansion. Software names are shown as learning areas and do not imply endorsement, partnership, accreditation, or authorization by the software vendors.
