SIFA CREDENTIALS

Demonstrate What You Know. Show What You Can Do.

SIFA Global Institute provides practical learning and assessment pathways designed to help learners demonstrate knowledge and practical skills in accounting technology, financial systems, and digital professional tools.

OUR APPROACH

Certification Should Reflect Practical Learning

At SIFA, certification is connected to learning, practice, assessment, and demonstrated understanding. Our goal is to make credentials meaningful by connecting them to practical skills rather than simply attendance.

01

Certificate of Completion

Awarded to learners who successfully complete a designated SIFA training program according to the applicable course requirements.

  • Structured learning program
  • Required learning activities
  • Course completion requirements
  • Certificate issued according to SIFA procedures
02

Certificate of Proficiency

Designed for programs that include an assessment of practical knowledge and skills in a defined subject area.

  • Defined learning objectives
  • Practical exercises
  • Knowledge and skills assessment
  • Performance evaluated against SIFA requirements
03

Future Professional Certification

SIFA plans to develop additional professional certification pathways as its programs, assessment systems, partnerships, and institutional capacity continue to grow.

  • Advanced assessments
  • Specialized technology skills
  • Professional development pathways
  • Future industry-aligned programs
LEARNING TO CREDENTIAL

Learn. Practice. Apply. Demonstrate.

SIFA connects its educational process to practical outcomes. Learners first understand the concepts, then practice them, apply them in realistic situations, and where applicable demonstrate their knowledge or skills through assessment.

01

Learn

Understand the accounting, financial, or technology concepts behind the subject.

02

Practice

Work through guided exercises using realistic examples and simulated business situations.

03

Apply

Use the knowledge and tools to complete practical tasks and solve relevant problems.

04

Demonstrate

Where required, demonstrate knowledge or practical proficiency through SIFA's applicable assessment process.

ASSESSMENT

Practical Assessment Principles

SIFA aims to make assessment relevant to the skills learners are expected to use in practical professional environments.

Depending on the program, assessment activities may include practical exercises, assignments, projects, quizzes, case studies, demonstrations, or other appropriate evaluation methods.

What Assessment Can Evaluate

  • Conceptual understanding
  • Accuracy of accounting processes
  • Software operation
  • Financial data handling
  • Problem-solving ability
  • Practical task completion
  • Interpretation of financial information

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

Understanding SIFA Certificates

SIFA Global Institute certificates represent the completion of and/or demonstrated proficiency within specific SIFA training and assessment requirements.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, SIFA certificates do not constitute a government-issued professional license, university degree, statutory professional qualification, or legal authorization to practice a regulated profession.

Any future professional certification, accreditation, institutional partnership, or externally recognized credential will be communicated only when the relevant arrangement or recognition has actually been established.

PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT

Build Skills. Build Evidence. Build Your Future.

Growth
