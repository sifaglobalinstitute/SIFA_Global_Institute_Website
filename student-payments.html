Monthly payment deadline: Tuition is due on the first day of each month.

Grace period: Payment may be made through the 14th day of that same month.

If the required tuition balance remains unpaid after the grace period, access to SIFA's protected learning services is suspended until the payment issue is resolved and payment is verified.

Renewals: Monthly payments are handled manually by SIFA staff. No automatic recurring charge is taken.

Scholarships and fee support

SIFA offers scholarship support only when an opportunity is officially announced. Scholarship information is public and can be read without signing in.

Full-funded scholarship

100%

Covers 100% of eligible course tuition for the period and under the conditions confirmed by SIFA.

50% scholarship

50%

Covers half of eligible course tuition. The student remains responsible for the other half of monthly tuition.

Certificate fee

The optional certificate fee is not covered by either scholarship type. It is charged separately only if the student requests a certificate after meeting the completion requirements.

Current scholarship status: No scholarship opportunity is currently open for applications. SIFA will publish the eligibility requirements, deadlines, and application instructions when an opportunity officially opens.
Payment deadline for scholarship students: The same monthly deadline and grace period apply to any remaining tuition balance. Any required balance is due on the 1st of the month, with a grace period through the 14th. If the required balance remains unpaid after the grace period, access to protected learning services is suspended until the issue is resolved and payment is verified.

Frequently asked questions

Can I read the payment and scholarship information without logging in?

Yes. This information page is public. Signing in is required for private student records, invoices, and submitting a receipt tied to your account.

How long is a course, and do I pay three months upfront?

Courses have a minimum duration of three months, but tuition is paid monthly. The three-month amount shown on this page is the total for three months, not a requirement to pay all three months upfront.

How much is tuition in my region?

Students in Rwanda pay 10,000 RWF per month. Students in other African countries pay $15 per month. Students in the rest of the world pay $25 per month. Check your invoice or contact SIFA if you need confirmation of the applicable fee.

When is monthly tuition due?

Monthly tuition is due on the 1st day of each month. The grace period continues through the 14th day of that same month.

What happens if I do not pay by the end of the grace period?

If the required tuition balance remains unpaid after the 14th, access to SIFA's protected learning services is suspended until the payment issue is resolved and payment is verified.

Do scholarship students also have a payment deadline?

Yes. The same monthly deadline and grace period apply to any tuition balance that remains after a scholarship is applied.

What do the full-funded and 50% scholarships cover?

A full-funded scholarship covers 100% of eligible course tuition. A 50% scholarship covers half of eligible course tuition. The exact period and conditions are confirmed by SIFA.

Does a scholarship cover the certificate fee?

No. The optional certificate fee is separate and is not covered by either scholarship type. The fee is charged only if you request a certificate after meeting SIFA's completion requirements: 5,000 RWF in Rwanda, $5 in other African countries, or $10 in the rest of the world.

Are scholarship applications open now?

No. There is currently no open scholarship opportunity. SIFA will publish details when an opportunity officially opens.

Does submitting a receipt automatically activate my course?

No. An authorized SIFA reviewer must verify and approve the payment. Uploading a receipt alone does not mark an invoice as paid or automatically activate learning access.

What if I need to pay in a different currency?

Confirm the correct invoice currency and amount with SIFA before making the transfer. Do not assume an exchange rate or calculate a conversion yourself.

Where can I verify the official payment details?

Email sifaglobalinstitute12@gmail.com or use the Contact page. Verify the recipient and amount before transferring money.
