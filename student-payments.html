Monthly payment deadline: Tuition is due on the first day of each month.

Learning access: Current verified payment or waiver and classroom authorization are required. An authorized temporary exception may apply. Follow your invoice deadline.

Renewals: Monthly payments are handled manually by SIFA staff. No automatic recurring charge is taken.

Scholarships and fee support

SIFA offers scholarship support only when an opportunity is officially announced. Scholarship information is public and can be read without signing in.

Full-funded scholarship

100%

Covers 100% of eligible course tuition for the period and under the conditions confirmed by SIFA.

50% scholarship

50%

Covers half of eligible course tuition. The student remains responsible for the other half of monthly tuition.

Certificate fee

The optional certificate fee is not covered by either scholarship type. It is charged separately only if the student requests a certificate after meeting the completion requirements.

Scholarship availability: Sign in to the Student Portal to check current application availability and eligibility.
Payment deadline for scholarship students: Pay any remaining balance by your invoice deadline. A fully waived invoice requires no tuition payment.

Frequently asked questions

Can I read fees and scholarships without signing in?

Yes. Public information is available to everyone. Your invoices, receipts and scholarship decisions require a verified student account.

How long are programs, and must I pay upfront?

Programs last one, two or four months. Tuition is billed monthly, not as a compulsory upfront payment for the whole program.

How much is tuition in my region?

Monthly published tuition is 10,000 RWF in Rwanda, 15 USD in other African countries and 25 USD in the rest of the world. Follow your issued invoice.

How do I apply and track my admission?

Sign in to the Student Portal, open My Application and apply when admissions for your program are available. You can track your decision and reference there.

When is tuition due?

Monthly tuition is normally due on the first day of the month. Follow the due date on your issued invoice. A payment grace period does not automatically grant classroom access.

What if my tuition remains unpaid?

Protected learning access requires current financial eligibility. Contact SIFA if you need an authorized temporary exception; do not assume access is guaranteed.

How can I check a scholarship decision?

Open My Application or Scholarships & Fee Waivers in My Fees & Payments. Awards and waived invoices are displayed separately.

What do 100% and 50% scholarships cover?

A 100% award waives eligible monthly tuition; a 50% award reduces eligible tuition by half. Only the approved period and conditions apply.

Do scholarship students have a payment deadline?

Students with a partial award must pay the remaining balance by the invoice deadline. A fully waived invoice requires no tuition payment.

Does a scholarship cover the certificate fee?

The optional certificate fee is separate: 5,000 RWF in Rwanda, 5 USD in other African countries and 10 USD in the rest of the world, subject to completion requirements.

Can I apply for a scholarship now?

Check My Application in the Student Portal for current availability and eligibility. SIFA announces opportunities officially.

Does submitting a receipt activate my course?

No. Authorized staff must verify the receipt. Enrollment and classroom authorization must also be in place.

When can I access my classroom?

An eligible enrollment, staff classroom authorization and current verified payment or waiver (or a valid authorized temporary exception) are required.

Can I pay in another currency?

SIFA supports RWF, TRY and USD. Use the invoice currency or obtain an approved payment quote before paying in a different currency.

Where can I see invoices and payment records?

Sign in and open My Fees & Payments. Review unpaid, pending, paid, rejected and waived records before paying again.

Where can I verify official payment details?

Contact SIFA at sifaglobalinstitute12@gmail.com or use the Contact page. Verify the recipient and amount before transferring money.
