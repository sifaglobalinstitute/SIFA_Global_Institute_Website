Privacy & Data Protection

Protecting the information you share with SIFA.

This page explains, in clear terms, how SIFA Global Institute may collect, use, protect and communicate about personal information provided through this website and its learning and application processes.

Our Commitment

We treat personal information responsibly.

SIFA Global Institute aims to collect only information that is reasonably needed to provide education, process applications, communicate with learners and applicants, and operate its services.

Purpose

Information may be used to process applications, assess training needs, provide learner support, manage enrolment, communicate important updates and deliver relevant SIFA services.

Limited Use

SIFA does not intend to sell personal information to third parties. Information should only be used for legitimate SIFA activities, administration, communication and service delivery.

Responsible Handling

SIFA aims to use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss or disclosure.

Information We May Collect

What you may provide to SIFA

Contact Information

For example, your name, email address, telephone number, country and other contact details you voluntarily provide.

Education & Application Information

Information such as educational background, selected program, professional interests, experience and other details submitted through an application.

Supporting Documents

Where an application requires documents, files such as identification or education records may be submitted for application and enrolment purposes.

Applications & Documents

Information submitted through the application process

Applicants should provide accurate information and should only upload documents that they are authorized to submit.

Important: SIFA's application process may involve personal information and supporting documents. Such information should be accessed only by people who need it for legitimate application, administration, learner-support or related SIFA purposes. Specific third-party processing arrangements, including any application-form service used by SIFA, will be communicated or reflected in the relevant application process when applicable.
Communication

How SIFA may contact you

SIFA may use the contact information you provide to respond to enquiries, communicate about an application, provide important course information, or send service-related updates.

Application Updates

Applicants may receive messages concerning their application, documents, eligibility, next steps or enrolment.

Learning Information

Learners may receive information related to schedules, classes, learning activities, assessments or certificates.

Important Notices

SIFA may contact users when communication is necessary for the operation or administration of a service they requested.

Third-Party Services

External platforms and service providers

SIFA may use external platforms to support website hosting, forms, communications, online learning or other services. When such services process personal information, their own terms and privacy practices may also apply.

Application forms: If SIFA uses a third-party form or application service such as Formspree, information submitted through that service may be processed by the service provider according to its applicable terms and privacy practices. SIFA will only connect such services when they are appropriate for the intended application workflow.
Your Responsibility

Please protect your own information too.

Do not send passwords, banking PINs, authentication codes or other highly sensitive information through ordinary website forms or email unless SIFA specifically provides a secure process for doing so.

Questions or Requests

Contact SIFA about your information

If you have a question about information you have submitted to SIFA, or you want to ask about how your information is handled, contact SIFA directly.
