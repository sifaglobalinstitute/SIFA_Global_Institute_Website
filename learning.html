Learning Experience

Learn. Practice. Apply.

SIFA's learning model is built around practical demonstrations, guided exercises, independent work, assessment and realistic projects.

1

Theory

Understand the accounting or business concept behind the task.

2

Demonstration

Watch the instructor connect the concept to the software.

3

Guided Practice

Perform the task with structured support.

4

Independent Practice

Complete a realistic task independently.

5

Assessment

Show that the defined competency has been achieved.

6

Project

Apply multiple skills to a simulated business environment.

Delivery

Online, practical and accessible.

SIFA's learning programs are designed to support live online classes, recorded resources, practical files, Q&A sessions, and digital learning materials.

Specific platforms such as Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams or other tools may change according to cost, scale and learner needs.
